# Smile Sanctuary Project

Create a modern, professional, and trust-building website for a dental clinic named Advanced Dental Hub.
The website should focus on patient confidence, easy appointment booking, and clearly showcasing the dentist’s expertise.

🎨 Design & Branding

Clean, medical, and premium design

Color palette: white, light blue, teal, and soft grey

Professional dental clinic vibe (hygiene, trust, care)

Mobile-responsive and fast loading

Use professional dental illustrations/icons (no cartoons)



---

🏠 Home Page

Include:

Hero section with headline:
“Advanced Dental Hub – Caring for Your Smile with Precision & Trust”

Sub-headline:
Advanced dental care using modern technology and expert hands.

Primary CTA buttons:
“Book Appointment” and “View Treatments”

Clinic highlights:

Painless treatments

Advanced equipment

Experienced dentist

Affordable & transparent pricing




---

👨‍⚕️ About Dentist / Expertise Section

Create a section titled “Meet Our Dentist”:

Dentist profile card with photo placeholder

Qualifications (BDS / MDS or equivalent)

Years of experience

Specializations:

Root Canal Treatment

Dental Implants

Cosmetic Dentistry

Braces & Aligners

Teeth Whitening

Pediatric Dentistry


Short trust-focused bio highlighting patient care and precision



---

🦷 Services / Treatments Page

Display services in clean cards with icons:

General Dentistry

Root Canal Treatment

Dental Implants

Braces & Aligners

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth Whitening

Gum Treatment

Pediatric Dentistry


Each service should have:

Short explanation

Benefits

“Book Appointment” button



---

📅 Appointment Booking System

Include a simple, user-friendly booking form:

Patient Name

Phone Number

Email (optional)

Preferred Date

Preferred Time

Treatment Type (dropdown)

Message / Problem description


After submission:

Show confirmation message:
“Thank you! Our team will contact you shortly to confirm your appointment.”
